The GB-220 gearbox on the BC-Q-26041 conveyor drive keeps running hot. Is it undersized for the 600 kg pallets?
BC-Q-26041-drive-spec.pdfPDF · 1.8 MBgearbox-nameplate.jpgJPG · 640 KB
[Assistant name]
I checked the spec, the gearbox data sheet and the motor data sheet. The gearbox is not undersized on torque, but it is close on the output shaft load.
What I found
  • The drive needs 380 N·m at the output shaft for 600 kg pallets at 0.35 m/s with a 1.5 service factor .
  • The GB-220 is rated 420 N·m continuous, so torque has 10 percent margin .
  • The radial load from the chain is 3,050 N (A). The limit is 3,200 N, so that margin is 4.7 percent .
  • The motor is rated 4.0 kW with a 1.15 service factor, which does not limit this drive .
Likely cause
Heat is more likely from the thin margin on shaft load and the 1,450 rpm input than from torque. Check the oil level and the sprocket pitch diameter first.

[Assistant name]
Radial load is chain tension divided by sprocket radius effects, so a larger sprocket lowers it. With the same 380 N·m, a pitch diameter of 270 mm gives a chain pull of 2,815 N (A). I need the chain size and the sprocket tooth count to give you a number I can stand behind.
