# **01** Executive Summary

**Design Engineering AI is the first turnkey app purpose-built for multi-disciplinary design engineering.** It is a software product developed for and funded by Block Concepts LLC, a Kansas custom automation company formed in July 2023. It starts with automation design; other design industries are possible once that core is functioning.

**Origin.** Grant Block spent his own time setting up general AI tools (environment settings, instructions, and output expectations) to make them useful for design engineering work at Block Concepts. Customers shouldn't have to do that. Design Engineering AI arrives with the setup already done.

**The problem.** Project and equipment knowledge is scattered across emails, printouts, ERP notes, spreadsheets, shared drives, and text messages. Work still gets done, but it costs time, quality, and worker well-being. In one example, a day-shift update never reached night shift, so equipment that was cleared to run sat idle and a full 8-hour shift was lost.

**The product.** One workspace per project where an AI assistant answers from the project's own files and data sheets: document search, a 3D viewer, project document storage, notes and tracking, engineering calculators, process flows, and generated plans and vendor requests. The next milestone is generating simple part models and drawings, so a maintenance technician can hand purchasing a drawing of a spacer.

**Customers.** Maintenance technicians first, then design engineers and engineering managers, on technical teams of typically 12 or fewer. That team may support a small operation or a large one that runs a small technical staff. First industries are food and beverage, packaging, and material handling, starting in the Midwest. The U.S. has about 538,000 industrial maintenance workers and 293,000 mechanical engineers.

**Business model.** Five levels: Basic Viewer (free), one-time Starter bundles that unlock up to 3 projects for a Basic Viewer account, Pro (\$30 per user per month), Team (\$50 per user per month: Pro plus an admin panel to control access), and Enterprise. Usage runs on a prepaid wallet, with storage add-ons; Enterprise contracts (flat fee plus memberships and integrations) are delivered through Block Concepts. A Turbo level for power users who need more speed than Pro, without Team admin controls or Enterprise cost, follows after launch. Two extensions are added to Pro after the MVP at \$600 per seat per year each: Design, a chat-based 3D designer, and Builder, which lets an organization package technical documentation for each of its clients into zip files opened in Lite, a free downloadable app that runs locally on an on-device AI model, with no login and no token cost. Enterprise is a custom implementation built on the Builder app.

**Position.** Existing tools split into AI for engineers inside CAD and AI for maintenance teams. Design Engineering AI brings both together for teams that include non-CAD staff.

**Team.** Grant Block and Isabel Wyndrum, owners and directors of Block Concepts, supported by advisors from contracting, automation supply, material handling and robotics, and accounting. The first planned hire is a senior software engineer.

**Founder.** Grant Block is a mechanical engineer and automation integrator based in the Kansas City metro, with more than 15 years in mechanical design and automation engineering. He holds a BS in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Kansas and served as Senior Mechanical Engineer at Preco, LLC, leading the design of OEM and custom die-cutting equipment on projects from \$100K to \$3M across films, battery membranes, adhesives, laminates, foams, antennas, solar panels, and medical devices. Since 2023 he has owned and engineered for Block Concepts LLC, delivering automation process development, digital twin modeling, vision inspection, and machine tending, while representing automation manufacturers through Automation Components. He is a member of A3, ISA, AWS, and ASME.

**Status and plan.** The minimum viable product is being built from November 2026 to March 2027, with public launch planned for April 2027. Running costs are about \$60–120 per month during the build and about \$265–325 per month after launch.

**Financial outlook.** At a target pace of 12 new Pro users per month, the product breaks even over three years while supporting a shared owner draw. At a slower pace, Block Concepts continues to fund the difference. AI usage (token) cost per task has not been measured yet and is the plan's largest open assumption; Section 8.4 sets out how it is handled and tested.

**Funding.** The product is self-funded by Block Concepts today. Outside funding may be considered later to support the first hire, with loan and grant options weighed first; any structure and terms would be decided at that time.

Usage (chat and CAD) is paid from a prepaid wallet at 3.5× cost. Pro includes 20 GB of storage. Basic Viewer includes no storage and mainly supports viewing and general chat, with a small daily AI cap; a one-time Starter bundle unlocks up to 3 projects of its own with a file upload limit, and Pro unlocks the full storage. Tokens are a separate wallet purchase, used for chat such as troubleshooting and vendor requests beyond the daily cap. Additional Pro storage is sold in 20 GB blocks (\$10). Each user has one license; collaborator mode lets users work together on shared projects.

Builder and Design (the 3D designer) are extensions added to Pro, each licensed per seat at \$600 per year (A); both ship after the MVP. Builder renewal includes software updates, package-format updates, and support. Packages already built still open in Lite if a subscription lapses; building new packages requires an active seat.

Bottom-up and technician-first. A maintenance technician joins as a Basic Viewer to see a shared project, adds a Starter bundle to set up projects for their own equipment, and shows the design engineers. ## 8.4 AI Usage (Token) Expectations: Key Unknown

|                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Token cost is the largest unknown in this plan.** The cost of each AI task has not been measured yet. Paid usage is protected because users prepay a wallet at 3.5× cost, but Basic Viewer usage and the wallet spend assumption are not. These figures are set in MVP testing before launch (November 2026 – March 2027). |

**What a token is.** AI providers bill by the token, a small piece of text (roughly three-quarters of a word). Every question, uploaded document the AI reads, and answer it writes uses tokens. Price per token varies by AI model, so the same task can cost several times more on a larger model.

**What drives usage per task**

| **Task**                     | **What drives token use**                                  | **Cost per task**          |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Chat question**            | Length of the question, answer, and conversation history   | To be measured (MVP)       |
| **Document search**          | Number and size of files the AI reads to answer            | To be measured (MVP)       |
| **Data sheet / manual read** | Page count of PDFs and data sheets loaded                  | To be measured (MVP)       |
| **Plan or vendor request**   | Length of the generated document and the source files used | To be measured (MVP)       |
| **Part model and drawing**   | Number of generation and correction steps                  | To be measured (after MVP) |

**Who carries the cost**

| **User**                          | **Who pays for tokens**                                                                               | **Exposure to Block Concepts**                                        |
|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pro**                           | User, from a prepaid wallet at 3.5× cost                                                              | Low: usage is paid before it is used                                  |
| **Basic Viewer**                  | Block Concepts, within a small daily cap                                                              | Medium: low per user, but grows with the number of Basic Viewer users |
| **Basic Viewer + Starter bundle** | User, from the bundle's prepaid tokens                                                                | Low: usage is paid before it is used                                  |
| **Lite / Builder**                | Lite runs on an on-device AI model; any Builder packaging AI use is paid from the subscriber's wallet | None: Lite uses no Block Concepts tokens                              |
| **Enterprise**                    | Set per contract                                                                                      | Priced in the contract                                                |
| **Development testing**           | Block Concepts                                                                                        | Capped at \$50–100 per month (Phase 0)                                |

**How it gets measured and controlled**

- **Log every task during the MVP:** tokens in, tokens out, model used, and cost, by task type.

- **Hard spending cap** at the AI provider during development and at launch.

- **Set the Basic Viewer daily cap and wallet pricing** from measured averages before launch, then review monthly.

- **Choose models by task:** smaller, lower-cost models for routine questions; larger models only where quality requires it.

- **Report monthly:** average cost per task, wallet spend per Pro user, and total Basic Viewer cost (key metric, Lean Canvas).
