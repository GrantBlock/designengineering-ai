## Product and money system (stated)

| Level or product | Price | What it gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Viewer | $0 | View, comment and ask questions on shared projects (paid by the owner's wallet); very small general-chat cap from the free pool; no own projects; no storage |
| Starter | $20 / year | 3 projects, 5 GB (100 MB per file) (A), one-time $5 token credit (A); tokens otherwise from the wallet, no limit; Design by tokens on a slow queue (about a week) |
| Pro | $30 / user / month | Projects and project chat, 20 GB (500 MB per file (A)), 10 active viewers per user, can buy extensions |
| Team | $50 / user / month | Pro plus admin and the Team settings section; 10 active viewers per user; live from year 2 |
| Turbo | [$X] | After infrastructure upgrades |
| Enterprise | Custom | Built on the Builder app |
| Design (extension) | $600 / seat / year | Chat-only 3D designer; after the MVP |
| Builder (extension) | $600 / seat / year | Desktop only; packages an organization's selected projects into Lite; unlimited view-and-chat viewers (fair use); Also share online (off by default) |
| Lite | Free | Offline package viewer with on-device chat; phones use the online copy in the main app |

Loss control: all free usage draws from one pool capped at 5% of Pro membership revenue; abuse and loss controls are in App Plan 6.4; the AI provider hard cap is the backstop. SBA 8.2.1: add-ons $61,300 over three years; remaining base-case gap $43,700.
