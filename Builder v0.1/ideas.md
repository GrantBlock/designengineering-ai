- I01 Draft (not yet approved by Grant): 2026-11-07: Use Liveblocks to mark system processes and components on a 3D model of a machine. Overlay an AI chat and markup of the system to help diagnose problems, for example an annotation on a component that says "crashed here" and recommends to maintenance that the automatic belt adjuster needs adjusting, based on a history of problems with it. It needs machine knowledge defined beforehand, because not every conveyor has those adjustments. It would also transfer knowledge from a senior to a younger staff member: a procedure created automatically, reviewed by the senior, then used by everyone else.
